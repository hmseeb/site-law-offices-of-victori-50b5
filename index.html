What you get when you bring your case to this office

Every matter starts with the outcome you need — charges answered, evidence tested, a dispute settled — and works backward into a plan you can follow.

Primary focus

Criminal defense — charges answered, accusations challenged

The goal is to put you in the strongest possible position against the accusation you are facing, including cases where the accusation is simply false.

Victoria Clemans defends people accused of crimes, from the first contact with investigators through hearings and trial. Complex cases are worked alongside outside specialists — including forensic audio and video experts — so that recordings, timelines and other evidence are examined rather than taken at face value.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I reach the office?

Call (310) 488-6357, email vclemansesquire@gmail.com, or send the form on this page. The office is at 401 Wilshire Blvd PH, Santa Monica, CA 90401.

What kinds of cases does Victoria Clemans handle?

Criminal defense, trial advocacy and litigation, and negotiation or conflict resolution — including complex cases that involve expert evidence such as forensic audio and video analysis.

What should I have ready when I call?

Whatever you already have: the charge or claim against you, any upcoming court date, the names of agencies or people involved, and any paperwork you were given. If you do not have any of it yet, call anyway and describe what happened.

Does contacting the office create an attorney-client relationship?

No. Sending a message through this website or leaving a voicemail does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship. Please avoid including sensitive details in a first message — share only enough for us to understand the matter, and we will confirm in writing if the office is representing you.

Can you promise a particular result in my case?

No, and no attorney can. Results depend on the facts, the evidence and the court. What this office can tell you is how the process works, what can be challenged, and what the realistic options are for your matter.

Which areas does the office serve?

The practice is based in Santa Monica and serves clients across the surrounding Los Angeles area. If you are not sure whether your matter is in range, call (310) 488-6357 and ask.

Three things reviewers keep raising

Complex cases, taken seriously

A forensic audio and video expert who has worked with the office “on several complex cases” is among the reviewers — a reminder that technical evidence in a criminal case is something to be examined, not accepted.

Advocacy in the room

Reviewers describe “split second timing and knowledge of the law” and being a “great communicator and advocate.” Those are courtroom qualities, and they are the reason trial preparation and negotiation are handled together rather than separately.

Someone on your side

“Victoria is the person I recommend to anyone who is seeking help and to be on your side,” one review reads. Being accused of something is isolating; having an advocate changes that.
